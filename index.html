Who We Are

About APEX

United War Command, operating under the tag [APEX], is an elite War Tycoon faction built on discipline, coordination, and tactical superiority. We welcome dedicated players who are committed to the mission and loyal to the cause.

🎯 Our Mission

To dominate the battlefield through strategic coordination, strong leadership, and unwavering teamwork. Every member is a vital part of the machine.

🛡️ Our Values

Respect, loyalty, and discipline define APEX. We hold our members to a high standard both in combat and in conduct.

📈 Advancement

Active and skilled members are recognized and promoted. Show up, perform, and climb the ranks of the United War Command.

🤝 Community

We're more than a faction — we're a community. Build friendships, sharpen your skills, and compete at the highest level.

Code of Conduct

Faction Rules

All members of APEX are expected to uphold these standards at all times.

01

Be Respectful

Show respect to everyone in the server. Harassment, bullying, or rude behavior won't be tolerated.

02

Follow Chain of Command

Always follow orders from superior officers. Disputes must be raised through proper channels, not ignored or bypassed.

03

No Betrayal or Leaking

Sharing APEX strategies, plans, or internal information with enemies or outsiders is grounds for immediate removal.

04

Stay Active

Inactive members may be demoted or removed. Notify leadership if you will be away for an extended period.

05

Represent APEX Professionally

Your behavior reflects on the whole faction. Act with discipline and integrity whenever the [APEX] tag is visible.

06

Promotion Is Earned

Ranks are awarded based on activity, performance, loyalty, and leadership ability — not seniority alone.
